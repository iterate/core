# Lend your browser or laptop, and give the mind a character

The owner should come away with: their devices can lend capabilities on demand (`iterate use-my-computer`, `iterate tunnel`, Chrome extension), their mind can have a distinct voice, and it knows how to reach them while they are away.

## How

Follow `onboarding/computer.md`, `onboarding/voice.md`, and `onboarding/reach.md`:

- Offer to connect their browser or computer, pick a character/voice for `mind.md`, and test their preferred notification channel (mail, Telegram, Slack, etc.).
- Mark this card `done`.
