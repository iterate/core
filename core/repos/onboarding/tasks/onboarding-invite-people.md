# Invite someone else to your mind or a Meet call

The owner should come away with: a partner, colleague, or friend can join as a member of the mind, or drop into a Meet call as a guest.

## How

Follow `onboarding/people.md`:

- Explain the difference between a member (passkey, full mind access) and a Meet guest (time-limited share link via `itx.config.shareAgent`).
- Link them to the members page when they are ready to invite someone, then mark this card `done`.
